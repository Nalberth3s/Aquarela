## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, contemporary, and poetic visual atmosphere for an early childhood education school. It bridges maternal security, refined pedagogy, and joyful discovery without relying on harsh primary colors or outdated early-2000s cartoon aesthetics.

The visual style blends **Soft Tactile Humanism** with **Modern Editorial Airiness**. Inspired by watercolor pigments blending gently into textured cotton paper, surfaces employ subtle transparency, ultra-diffused atmospheric glows, and generous breathing room. The UI evokes serenity, intellectual care, and heartfelt hospitality—reassuring parents while honoring the wonder of childhood through sophisticated, organic nuances.

## Colors

The palette mimics wet-on-wet watercolor washes across textured paper. High-saturation rainbow palettes are strictly avoided in favor of tonal harmony between airy pastel tints and grounding pigments.

- **Primary (`#3B82A6` - Céu Sereno):** A gentle cerulean blue representing trust, guidance, and peaceful routine. Paired with background tint `#E8F1F5`.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6` - Flor de Lavanda):** A delicate lilac conveying imagination, sensitivity, and poetic care. Paired with background tint `#F3EFF8`.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Sol Poente):** A warm, radiant amber evoking warmth and morning curiosity. Paired with background tint `#FEF6E4`.
- **Accents:**
  - **Sálvia Criativa:** Base `#10B981` with surface wash `#EDF5EE` for outdoor activities and health indicators.
  - **Coral Afeto:** Base `#F43F5E` with surface wash `#FDF2F0` for alerts, urgent reminders, and loving highlights.
- **Neutrals:**
  - **Canvas Base:** `#FAF9F6` (Alabastro/Papel de Algodão), warm and eye-resting.
  - **Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` with slight translucency.
  - **Text Neutral Deep:** `#232B38` for primary readability.
  - **Text Neutral Muted:** `#627282` for secondary metadata and instructional prompts.

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines, editorial narratives, operational data, and micro-copy. Its geometric clarity combined with wide aperture and humanist soft curves makes it exceptionally welcoming without forfeiting institutional authority.

- **Weight Discipline:** Restrict styling to weights `400` (Regular), `500` (Medium), `600` (Semi-bold), and `700` (Bold). Avoid extra bold weights that disrupt the delicate watercolor harmony.
- **Editorial Fluidity:** Display and Headline-LG utilize tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.02em`) to produce a bespoke, crafted poise.
- **Readability:** Body typography leverages expansive line heights (1.6x to 1.7x) to preserve calm scanning for busy parents.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced, fluid 12-column grid for desktop environments (max container width 1240px) that simplifies into a 6-column grid for tablets and a 4-column stack on mobile devices.

Generous vertical padding between sections creates a sense of breathing room, preventing the cognitive overload common in scholastic and administrative platforms. Components and content containers float comfortably within consistent page margins, anchoring information while maintaining an open, sunlit aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Washes** and **Watercolor Dispersion Shadows** rather than stark drop-shadows or heavy structural borders.

- **Surface Layers:**
  - Base canvas sits on `#FAF9F6`.
  - Floating content cards use `#FFFFFF` with 90% opacity over soft backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **The Watercolor Glow (Ambient Shadow):**
  Shadows are multi-layered, widely diffused, and tinted with soft ambient hues (sky blue and slate) rather than cold gray or pure black:
  - *Standard Card:* `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(59, 130, 166, 0.08), 0 2px 10px -2px rgba(35, 43, 56, 0.03);`
  - *Hover State:* `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -6px rgba(59, 130, 166, 0.14), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(35, 43, 56, 0.05);`
- **Edges:** Outlines are feather-light (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` or `1px solid rgba(59, 130, 166, 0.08)`), replicating the deckled edge of cold-press art paper.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts smooth, organic curvature inspired by pebbles, smoothed water droplets, and paint palettes. 

- **Containers & Big Surfaces:** Large cards, dialogs, and modals employ `rounded-3xl` (2rem to 2.5rem), providing a cradle-like, safe visual envelope.
- **Interactive Elements:** Buttons, search fields, pills, and informational tags leverage full pill shapes (`rounded-full`) or generous arcs (`rounded-2xl` / 1rem).
- **Asymmetric Flourishes:** Special promotional or gallery containers may feature subtle organic corner variations (e.g., `rounded-tl-3xl rounded-br-3xl rounded-tr-xl rounded-bl-xl`) to echo hand-painted brush shapes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background of `#3B82A6` with `#FFFFFF` text. Fully rounded pill, featuring subtle vertical color bleed (`linear-gradient(180deg, #448EB4 0%, #3B82A6 100%)`). Interactive hover lifts the button by 2px with an expanded cyan-blue watercolor glow.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Delicate pastel backgrounds (`#E8F1F5` or `#F3EFF8`) with matching deepened text (`#3B82A6` or `#8B5CF6`). Seamless, zero border, soft hover brightening.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure transparent background, neutral deep text, accompanied by an animated organic underline on hover.

### Cards
- Clean white cards with generous internal padding (`space-lg`), `rounded-3xl` corners, and an ambient watercolor drop-shadow.
- Header cards or milestone trackers may carry a subtle gradient bleed along the top border (e.g., 4px wash of lavender into warm sunlight).

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs rest on pure white or `#F5F6F8` background with an ultra-soft border (`rgba(35, 43, 56, 0.08)`), `rounded-2xl`, and internal padding `0.875rem 1.25rem`.
- Focus ring displays an atmospheric wash of `#3B82A6` at 20% opacity with no harsh dark outlines.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped tags with paired watercolor tints:
  - *Pedagogia/Atividades:* `#E8F1F5` background with `#2C6986` text.
  - *Avisos & Afeto:* `#FDF2F0` background with `#C2314D` text.
  - *Alimentação/Bem-estar:* `#EDF5EE` background with `#0E7E58` text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded, soft-cornered check squares (`rounded-lg`) and circular radios. Selected states transition gently to a saturated primary or secondary fill with an animated micro-scale checkmark.

### Bespoke School Components
- **Diário de Bordo (Daily Timeline):** A soft vertical timeline tethered by a dashed line resembling running watercolor thread, marking events (nap time, culinary workshop, garden exploration) with pastel badge anchors.
- **Aquarela Avatar:** Profile avatars encapsulated in fluid, slightly uneven organic circular masks rather than rigid geometric cuts.
